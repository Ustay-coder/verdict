# Acme Robotics — Seed Pitch

## Problem
Warehouse operators lose 11% of picker time to walking between aisles. For a 200-person site that is $1.4M/year.

## Solution
Autonomous carts that follow pickers and shuttle totes to packing, cutting walking by 60%.

## Traction
- 3 paying customers, $42k MRR, 100% logo retention over 9 months
- 1,200 daily active carts across 4 sites; users report 38% faster picks

## Team
Two ex-Amazon Robotics engineers and a former DHL operations director.

## Ask
Raising $2.5M to reach 20 sites.
