  <!-- HOW -->
  <section id="how">
    <div class="wrap">
      <p class="kicker">How it works</p>
      <h2 class="t">Benchmarks aren't about your cases. Verdict is.</h2>
      <p class="sub">Decision models answer typed questions — yes or no, one of these, a level on a scale — with probabilities instead of prose, in a fraction of a second. Every vendor publishes benchmarks, and each wants its questions in its own shape.</p>

      <div class="steps">
        <div class="step reveal">
          <span class="n">01</span>
          <h3>Write a rubric</h3>
          <p>Criteria, scales, levels — once, in one YAML file you keep under version control.</p>
<pre><span class="c"># rubric.yaml</span>
<span class="k">verdict</span> rubric init rubric.yaml</pre>
        </div>
        <div class="step reveal">
          <span class="n">02</span>
          <h3>Ask every model</h3>
          <p>Verdict turns the rubric into the questions each model takes and sends the same questions to all of them.</p>
<pre><span class="k">--models</span> jev,clef,openai,gemini
<span class="c"># one run per model, every run kept</span></pre>
        </div>
        <div class="step reveal">
          <span class="n">03</span>
          <h3>Read the disagreements</h3>
          <p>Answers side by side, with the cases where models differ pulled out. Compare any two runs case by case.</p>
<pre><span class="k">verdict</span> diff --base $BEFORE --head $AFTER
<span class="c"># the ids of any two runs</span></pre>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>

          <li><span>A model that cannot answer keeps its row and says why.</span></li>
