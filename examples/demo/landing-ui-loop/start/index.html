FAQ

Questions, answered.

How does Tideline avoid false alarms?+

When a check fails, we re-run it right away from at least three other regions. You're alerted only if a majority of them agree the service is down, so one flaky network path won't wake you.

Do you charge per user?+

No. Every paid plan includes unlimited teammates. Pricing is based on how many monitors you run and how often they check.

Can I migrate from my current tool?+

Yes. Import monitors from a CSV or from most popular uptime tools' export formats. Most teams finish migrating in less than ten minutes.

What happens when I hit my monitor limit?+

Your existing monitors keep running. We'll ask you to upgrade or archive a monitor before you add another. We never switch off monitoring without warning.

Is there an API?+

Yes. There's a full REST API, a CLI, and a Terraform provider. Anything you can do in the dashboard, you can also script.
