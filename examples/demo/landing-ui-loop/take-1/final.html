Cron-job monitoring

Each job pings a unique URL when it finishes. If a ping is missed, Tideline alerts you after a grace period you set.

# end of backup.sh curl -fsS https://hb.tideline.dev/your-job-id

Frequently asked questions

How does Tideline avoid false alarms?+

Every monitor is checked from 6 regions. You're alerted only when at least 2 of them agree the service is down, so one flaky network path won't wake you.

Do you charge per user?+

No. Pricing is based on how many monitors you run.

Can I migrate from my current tool?+

Yes. Import monitors from a CSV or from most popular uptime tools' export formats.

What happens when I hit my monitor limit?+

Your existing monitors keep running. We'll ask you to upgrade or archive a monitor before you add another. We never switch off monitoring without warning.

Is there an API?+

Yes. There's a full REST API, a CLI, and a Terraform provider. Anything you can do in the dashboard, you can also script.
