# Verdict

## AI-powered evaluation for your content

Verdict is an evaluation platform. Upload documents, websites or videos and get a score back, powered by
the latest AI models.

Teams use Verdict to review pitch decks, grade essays, screen sponsorship videos and triage support tickets.
Instead of spreadsheets and endless meetings, you get consistent results in seconds.

### Features

- **Any input.** PDFs, web pages, YouTube links, JSON and plain text.
- **Custom rubrics.** Decide what matters to your team.
- **Fast.** Results in seconds, not days.
- **Humans in the loop.** Your reviewers can step in when needed.
- **Developer friendly.** A REST API and SDK so you can build it into your product.

### How it works

1. Create a rubric.
2. Send your content.
3. Get a score.
