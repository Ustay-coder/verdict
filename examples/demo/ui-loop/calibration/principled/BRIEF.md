# Acme Pulse dashboard: brief

**Who and when.** The founder opens this every Monday morning, before the weekly team call. After reading it
they decide whether anything needs a call, an email, or a change to this week's plan.

**Questions, in priority order**
1. Does anything need my attention this week? Cancellations, and churn.
2. Is revenue growing? MRR and its change versus last month.
3. Is the funnel healthy? Weekly signups over the last 8 weeks, trial-to-paid conversion, net new accounts.

**What counts as "needs attention"** (rules on the data we have, no invented targets)
- Every `Cancelled` event is flagged, with the account and the stated reason.
- Churn (2.3%) is shown next to net new accounts (+37), so the founder can see whether growth outpaces losses.

**Left out, on purpose**
- Plan-level ARPA and per-plan percentages repeated in several places: shown once, in one plan table.
- A total-MRR donut: MRR already appears once in the revenue line.
- Decoration of any kind (icons, badges, gradients, motion).
