# Receipt Run (fictional hackathon entry)
Expense reports for small businesses: snap a receipt, it's categorised, matched to the card charge and exported to
the accounting tool. Every small business loses hours to this each month; across millions of them it adds up to a
huge cost. Built on a proven OCR API and the two most common accounting integrations; 40 pilot users at three
companies already submit every expense through it, and the demo runs end to end with real exports.
Honest caveat: expense apps are a crowded category, and this one works like the others.
