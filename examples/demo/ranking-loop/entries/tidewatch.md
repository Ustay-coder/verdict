# Tidewatch (fictional hackathon entry)
Street-level flood warnings from cheap water sensors on storm drains, meshed over LoRa, so a neighbourhood gets
a 20-minute warning before a street floods. No one does this at street level today, and it could protect
millions of homes. Right now it's one sensor prototype on a desk and a mock map; the mesh, the power budget and
the city permits are unsolved.
