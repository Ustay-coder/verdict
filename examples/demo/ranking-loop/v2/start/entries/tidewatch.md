# Tidewatch

🌊 Revolutionizing flood safety with AI.

Tidewatch is an AI-powered early-warning platform that predicts floods before they happen with 99% accuracy. Our smart sensor network already protects 12 neighbourhoods and could save millions of lives worldwide.

Using cutting-edge IoT and machine learning, Tidewatch delivers real-time alerts to anyone, anywhere. It is the future of disaster resilience.

We are passionate about making the world safer, and we can't wait to scale Tidewatch globally.
